Welcome to the University Information Knowledge Base — an extensive resource designed to assist students in all aspects of university life, from admissions to graduation. This document contains detailed information about academic programs, administrative procedures, campus services, student life, and digital tools available to all students.

---

### 1. About the University

The University is a public higher education institution dedicated to excellence in teaching, research, and community engagement. It offers a wide range of undergraduate, graduate, and professional programs across multiple faculties. Its mission is to foster innovation, critical thinking, and social responsibility in students.

The university operates on a semester-based academic calendar, typically divided into:

* **Fall Semester:** September to January
* **Spring Semester:** February to June
* **Summer Session (optional):** July to August

The official language of instruction is English (or French, depending on the faculty), and courses may include lectures, tutorials, labs, and project-based learning.

---

### 2. Faculties and Programs

The university is composed of several faculties, each offering specialized programs:

#### Faculty of Engineering

* Departments: Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Industrial Engineering.
* Degrees Offered: Bachelor of Engineering (B.Eng.), Master of Science (M.Sc.), and Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.)
* Notable programs: Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Renewable Energy Systems, Robotics, and Cloud Computing.

#### Faculty of Science

* Departments: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Earth Sciences.
* Programs emphasize both theoretical foundations and laboratory applications.
* Research labs and summer internships are available for top students.

#### Faculty of Business and Economics

* Degrees: Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA), Master of Business Administration (MBA), and specialized Master’s in Finance, Marketing, and Entrepreneurship.
* Students complete internships in companies, NGOs, or start-ups during their third or fourth year.

#### Faculty of Arts and Humanities

* Programs in Languages, History, Philosophy, Sociology, and Media Studies.
* Opportunities include exchange programs and cultural events hosted throughout the academic year.

#### Faculty of Medicine and Health Sciences

* Offers undergraduate medical education, nursing, pharmacy, and public health programs.
* Students complete practical training in affiliated hospitals.

### 3. Admission Process

The university admits students based on academic merit, entrance exams, and language proficiency.

**Undergraduate Admission Requirements:**

* High school diploma or equivalent.
* Official transcripts.
* Proof of language proficiency (TOEFL, IELTS, or DELF for French).
* Motivation letter and, in some programs, an interview.

**Graduate Admission Requirements:**

* Bachelor’s degree in a related field.
* CV/resume.
* Recommendation letters.
* Research proposal (for thesis-based programs).
* Language certificate.

**Application Timeline:**

* Fall intake: Applications open in **December** and close in **April**.
* Spring intake: Applications open in **June** and close in **October**.

Applicants can track their status on the **University Admission Portal** and receive notifications via email.

---

### 4. Tuition Fees and Financial Aid

Tuition fees vary depending on the program and level of study:

* Undergraduate programs: 1,500 – 3,000 USD per semester.
* Master’s programs: 2,000 – 4,000 USD per semester.
* Doctoral programs: 2,500 – 5,000 USD per semester (may be waived for research assistants).

**Scholarships and Grants:**

* **Merit-based Scholarships:** Awarded to students with exceptional academic performance.
* **Need-based Grants:** For students from low-income families.
* **Research Assistantships:** Offered to postgraduate students involved in funded projects.
* **Exchange Scholarships:** Available for students participating in international programs.

All scholarship applications are submitted through the **Student Financial Portal**.

---

### 5. Academic Regulations

* **Credit System:** Each course carries a number of credits; students must earn a minimum of 180 credits for a bachelor’s degree and 120 for a master’s.
* **Grading Scale:** A (90–100), B (80–89), C (70–79), D (60–69), F (below 60).
* **Attendance:** Minimum attendance of 75% is required for each course.
* **Examinations:** Midterm and final exams are mandatory. Some courses include project evaluations and continuous assessment.
* **Academic Integrity:** Plagiarism or cheating leads to disciplinary action, including suspension.

### 6. Digital Tools and Student Portals

Students have access to several online systems:

* **Student Information System (SIS):** For course registration, grades, and transcripts.
* **Learning Management System (LMS):** Hosts online lectures, assignments, and discussion forums.
* **University Email:** All official communication is sent to the university email address.
* **Library Portal:** Provides access to e-books, research databases (IEEE, Springer, ScienceDirect), and journals.
* **IT Helpdesk:** Offers technical support for account issues, software installation, and campus Wi-Fi.

### 7. Campus Life and Facilities

The university promotes a vibrant campus culture with a variety of clubs, organizations, and events.

**Student Services:**

* **Academic Advising Center:** Helps students plan their courses and academic path.
* **Career Services Office:** Provides internship guidance, CV reviews, and job fairs.
* **Health Center:** Offers free consultations and medical assistance.
* **Counseling and Psychological Services:** Provides mental health support and workshops.
* **Library:** Open 7 days a week with silent study zones and group rooms.
* **Sports Complex:** Includes gym, swimming pool, and sports fields.

**Clubs and Associations:**

* Robotics Club, Debate Society, Drama Club, Music Band, Student Union, Women in Tech, and Environmental Association.
* Students can propose new clubs by submitting a request to the Student Affairs Office.

---

### 8. Housing and Transportation

* **On-campus Housing:** Available for both local and international students. Rooms are single or shared with internet, laundry, and study areas.
* **Off-campus Housing:** The university assists in finding safe accommodation near the campus.
* **Transportation:** Shuttle buses connect the campus to nearby cities. Parking spaces are available for students with vehicles.

---

### 9. International Students

The university welcomes students from over 50 countries.

**Support Services:**

* Orientation week for newcomers.
* Assistance with residence permits and visas.
* Language support classes (English/French/Arabic).
* International Student Office that helps with adaptation and social integration.

**Exchange Programs:**

* Erasmus+, bilateral agreements with partner universities in Europe, Asia, and North America.
* Credits earned abroad are transferable through the ECTS system.

---

### 10. Research and Innovation

The university encourages research across all disciplines.

* **Research Centers:** Artificial Intelligence, Renewable Energy, Biotechnology, and Social Innovation.
* **Funding Opportunities:** Internal grants and collaborations with industry partners.
* **Undergraduate Research Program:** Enables students to work with professors on ongoing projects.
* **Innovation Lab:** Supports student start-ups through mentorship and seed funding.

---

### 11. Internship and Career Development

Internships are mandatory in most programs during the final year.

* Students can find internship offers through the **Career Portal**.
* The university has partnerships with over 200 national and international companies.
* Workshops on CV writing, job interviews, and entrepreneurship are held regularly.

Graduates benefit from a **Career Alumni Network** that connects them with employers and professional opportunities.

---

### 12. Student Rights and Responsibilities

Students are expected to:

* Respect academic integrity and the university code of conduct.
* Attend classes regularly and participate actively.
* Maintain ethical behavior on campus and online.
* Use university resources responsibly.

In return, students have the right to:

* Access quality education and fair assessment.
* Seek academic or psychological support.
* Participate in decision-making through student representation bodies.

---

### 13. Graduation and Alumni Network

* To graduate, students must complete all academic and financial requirements.
* A graduation ceremony is held every July.
* Graduates receive both a printed diploma and a digital certificate (verified via QR code).

The **Alumni Office** maintains contact with graduates and organizes events, mentoring programs, and networking sessions.
